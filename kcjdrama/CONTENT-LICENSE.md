# KCJDrama content license notes

Site prose in the mega-set (tropes, syndromes, essays, hubs, bingo) is
**original writing for kcjdrama.com**.

## Allowed on this site

- Original commentary, glossaries, satire, and trope definitions
- Short factual title mentions without plot lifts
- Original fiction inspired by *patterns*, not by copying a show

## Not allowed

- Official stills, posters, OST audio, subtitle files
- Scraped fanart of copyrighted characters
- Episode recaps that reproduce plots or long dialogue
- Piracy / illegal streaming directories
- Copy-paste from third-party recap or wiki sites

Theme code remains under the theme’s GPL-compatible WordPress theme norms.
